Disclaimer, contact and acknowledgements

About the data. This database consolidates literature values by chemical species, core level and peak assignment. Compatible measurements are combined into a representative binding energy and associated uncertainty. Only reported core levels are listed; their absence from the database does not imply their absence from a spectrum.

Energy calibration and fitting. Binding energies are presented using the energy calibration reported by each source. Calibration details are available with each reference. Reported peak positions may also depend on the constraints and procedures used to fit complex spectra.

Interpretation. Differences of a few tenths of an electronvolt may result from calibration, chemical environment, material structure or fitting procedure and should not alone be interpreted as evidence of distinct chemical states.

Scope. This database is intended as a reference aid. Although the entries have been compiled and internally reviewed with reasonable care, interpretation should always consider the original publication and its experimental context.

If you identify an error, have a question, or wish to suggest an addition, please contact Lénaïc Madec at lenaic.madec@cnrs.fr. Corrections and additions will be documented and incorporated into future releases of the database and its archived dataset.

We thank the authors of the cited work, as well as colleagues and users who contribute feedback and help improve this resource.
