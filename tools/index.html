XPS Analysis Tools

Tools scopeTools support iterative-acquisition review, dataset preparation, user-guided spectrum identification including calibration & comparison of spectral regions as well as figure creation. They are primarily developed for battery materials and SEI analysis. Tools and VAMAS files are processed locally in your browser.
Select an XPS analysis tool

XPS VAMAS Dataset Builder

Prepare and organize XPS datasets stored in VAMAS format from single or multiple files. Review iterative acquisitions, exclude unwanted spectra, and build a clean multi-sample dataset for later XPS processing.

Prepare & organize datasets →

XPS Spectrum Identification

Interpret XPS spectra from VAMAS files. Survey spectra can be analyzed in terms of possible elemental lines, while core-level regions can be compared with curated binding-energy literature references and reported peak assignments.

Explore & interpret spectra →

XPS Figure Builder

Create multi-panel XPS figures from CSV data with shared axes, filled components and annotations, then export them as images.

Build XPS figures →
Disclaimer, contact and acknowledgements

The tools provided here are intended to assist XPS data preparation and interpretation and should not be considered automated or definitive analysis methods.

Suggested outputs depend on the available reference data, spectrum quality, calibration, instrumental conditions and sample chemistry. Results should be critically evaluated by the user.

The tools are currently under development and may contain incomplete data, limitations or errors.

If you identify an issue, have a question, or wish to suggest an improvement, please contact Lénaïc Madec at lenaic.madec@cnrs.fr.

We thank the authors of the reference data used by these tools, as well as colleagues and users who contribute feedback and help improve this resource.
