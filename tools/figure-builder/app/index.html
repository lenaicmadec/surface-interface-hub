Figure Builder help PanelsClick to select. Drag to move. Arrow keys move by 1 px; Shift + arrow moves by 10 px. Text, legends and linesClick an object to edit it. Drag or use the arrow keys to reposition it. Draw a line or arrowChoose the ↗ tool, then drag on the figure. Hold Shift to constrain its angle. SnapObjects align to nearby edges, centres and annotations. Use the arrow keys for precise positioning without snapping. Copy to another panelSelect an annotation, press Ctrl/Cmd + C, select the destination panel, then press Ctrl/Cmd + V. DuplicateCtrl/Cmd + D creates a copy in the current panel or figure. DeleteDelete or Backspace removes the selected panel or annotation. Undo and redoCtrl/Cmd + Z undoes a change; Ctrl/Cmd + Y restores it. Automatic recoveryAn unsaved local draft can be restored after an accidental tab or browser closure.
