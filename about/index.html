About Surface & Interface Hub

About the project

Surface & Interface Hub is a personal scientific resource focused on the study of battery interfaces with a focus on the Solid Electrolyte Interphase (SEI) and X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy (XPS).

The project brings together educational resources, curated reference data and browser-based tools for research and teaching. Uploaded data are processed locally, and content is progressively expanded.

About the author

Lénaïc Madec is a CNRS researcher at the Institut des Matériaux Jean Rouxel (IMN) in Nantes. Since completing his Ph.D. in Physical Chemistry and Electrochemistry in 2012, he has studied interfacial and ageing mechanisms in Li-ion, K-ion and solid-state batteries, mainly using XPS, Auger spectroscopy and cross-section sample preparation. After postdoctoral research in Canada at Dalhousie University and within the French RS2E network, he joined the CNRS at IPREM (Pau) in 2017, then moved to IMN in 2023.
